Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

In short: your data stays on your phone.

Hisaab Kitaab ("the app") is an expense tracker and udhaar khata for Android. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

What the app stores

Entries you add (expenses, income, khata entries, bills, people's names and optional phone numbers, notes and amounts) and your settings are stored in a database on your phone. The app has no account, no sign-in and no server. We do not receive, see or collect this information.

Internet and tracking

This version of the app does not request internet access and has no analytics, advertising or tracking code. If advertising is added in a future version, this policy will be updated before that version is released.

Contacts

When you choose to pick a contact for a khata, Android shows its own contact picker and gives the app only the one contact you select (name and phone number). The app does not read your contact list.

Files you create

Balance sheet PDFs and backup files are created only when you ask for them. You decide where they go: saved on your phone, or shared through an app you choose (for example WhatsApp, email or Drive). Once you share a file, the receiving app's own privacy policy applies. Backups are not encrypted, so keep them somewhere you trust.

Notifications and permissions

The app asks to show notifications so it can remind you of bills. Reminders are scheduled on your phone. It also uses "receive boot completed" to set reminders again after a restart, and "vibrate" for the vibration option. Spoken reminders use your phone's own text-to-speech engine.

This website and tester sign-ups

This website does not use cookies for tracking or analytics. If you email us to join the testing program, we receive your name and email address through your own email app. We use them only to add you to the test on Google Play, to contact you about it and to send your certificate. We do not sell or share them.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Deleting your data

Your data is on your phone only. Uninstalling the app or clearing its storage deletes it. Backup files you saved or shared are separate; delete them yourself.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new date.
